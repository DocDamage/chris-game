HOW TO PLAY

The universe has crashed. Ordinary objects are hostile. You are underqualified. Survive 10 waves, beat the bosses, then keep going in Endless Mode.

KEYBOARD + MOUSE

CONTROLLER

TIPS

  • Red circles, lines and ! marks warn you before a hit lands. Move out of them.
  • Dodge rolling makes you invulnerable for a moment, but uses stamina and has a cooldown.
  • Gems give XP. Level up to choose 1 of 3 upgrades. Gold buys things at vending machines (press E, or B on a controller).
  • Two weapons at once: the main weapon is on the right trigger / left mouse button, the second on the left trigger / right mouse button.
  • Rubber chickens bounce. Pizza explodes. The baguette bats enemy shots (and arrows!) back at them.
  • A random chaos event hits every minute or so. They change the rules, so read the banner!
  • Kill streaks raise your score multiplier. Getting hit resets it. Bosses drop a token: grab it for a special reward.
  • Power-ups show the item that does the job: swords = Rage, boots = Haste, shields = Shield, potions = Focus / Regen, books = Scholar, an hourglass = Time Warp, a magnet pulls in loot, a bomb explodes, a chest pays out. Food and red potions heal.
  • Trophies: enemies drop items that fit them and bosses drop rare ones. Every one has an effect and goes into the Trophy Codex (main menu), where you can sell extras for scrap. Filling the codex unlocks permanent bonuses.
  • Co-op: two players share one screen while close and get a split screen when apart. Stand next to a downed partner to revive them.
